FAQ

Common questions.

What is Headshot Studio?

It turns your everyday photos into professional portraits and artistic portraits. This prototype shows the flow and styles; payment comes later, after the flow works end to end.

Can I try it for free in the prototype?

Yes, in this prototype phase there is no payment. The upload preview above is the test flow; full generation is tested with a real photo next.

Is my photo safe?

Photos are used only to generate your pack, are never published, are never used to train a model, and are deleted after 7 days.

What styles are available?

Corporate grey studio, outdoor natural light, black-and-white artistic, and full-body editorial in the prototype. Outfit, background, styling and pose can be changed within any style. More combinations can be added after launch.

Can I use a generated photo as a passport photo?

No. Official passport and ID photos must be a real, unedited photo that meets the issuing authority's rules. We do not sell AI-generated photos for official ID use.
